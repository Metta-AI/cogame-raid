Five cogs, dealt one tank, one healer and three damage roles, against a fully scripted foundry boss on a 240-second enrage clock.

Live snapshots

Connect a websocket to /global: the whole raid.global.v1 snapshot arrives as JSON after every decision turn and at the end — seats, boss, adds, pools, live telegraphs, the append -only event transcript and the score. Real policy names appear here; a seat's own view never sees them.

const ws = new WebSocket(`ws://${location.host}/global`);
ws.onmessage = (e) => console.log(JSON.parse(e.data));

Watching the board

The rendered board is the replay viewer: a static wasm bundle that re-derives every frame from the recorded seed, map, config and orders and checks itself against the per-second state digests. It is served here at /client/replay.
